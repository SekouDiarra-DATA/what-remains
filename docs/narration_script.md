# Narration script — ready to paste into Descript / CapCut text-to-speech

Each numbered block below is meant to become **one TTS-generated audio clip**, placed on your timeline aligned to the matching moment in `demo/what-remains-demo.mp4` (your existing silent recording, which followed this same click sequence). Approximate spoken length is noted so you can judge pacing — trim your video's pauses to match rather than stretching the audio.

Voice suggestion: a calm, measured, mid-register voice (not upbeat/energetic) — matches the product's sober tone. In Descript/CapCut, avoid "cheerful" or "promo" voice presets.

---

**Block 1 — Hook (~15s)**
> Every year, about 63 million people die worldwide. Every one of them leaves behind an active digital life — subscriptions, bank auto-payments, cloud storage — that keeps running with no one watching it.

**Block 2 — The real problem (~12s)**
> Studies show almost half of people have no plan at all for their digital accounts after death. The hardest part for the family left behind isn't cancelling those accounts — it's finding them in the first place.

**Block 3 — Solution, one line (~10s)**
*(Align to: the consent screen appearing)*
> This is What Remains: an agent that reads through a deceased person's email history and reconstructs exactly what's still active — so a grieving family doesn't have to.

**Block 4 — Consent & trust (~10s)**
*(Align to: the "what this tool never does" text, checkbox being checked)*
> It never sends anything or cancels anything on its own. Every action stays in the family's hands — including who the analysis is sent to, stated upfront.

**Block 5 — Loading the sample (~8s)**
*(Align to: expanding "Try a sample inbox", clicking "Load the sample email export")*
> For this demo, we're using a simulated, privacy-safe inbox instead of a real one. The real product connects a live Gmail account directly.

**Block 6 — The checklist (~10s)**
*(Align to: the results screen appearing, list visible)*
> Here's what it found — ordered by priority, and every account is clearly marked as confirmed or unconfirmed. Nothing is guessed at with false confidence.

**Block 7 — The core pivot: multi-email synthesis (~15s)**
*(Align to: opening Netflix or Spotify, showing the list of source emails)*
> This didn't just read one email. It built one account profile from several separate traces — a signup, a price increase, a payment — not a checklist of individual messages.

**Block 8 — Real cancellation steps (~8s)**
*(Align to: showing the numbered steps + official link for Netflix)*
> For well-known providers, it found the actual cancellation steps — not a generic template.

**Block 9 — Honest fallback + draft (~12s)**
*(Align to: opening FitZone Gym, clicking "Draft a message to their support")*
> When it can't find a verified procedure, it says so honestly — and drafts a message instead, which is never sent automatically. You review it, adapt it, send it yourself.

**Block 10 — Tangible deliverable (~10s)**
*(Align to: scrolling to footer, clicking "Download full report as PDF")*
> Everything — evidence, history, next steps, drafts — exports as one document the family can keep, or hand to a notary.

**Block 11 — Why the channel matters (~12s)**
> This only works because the agent lives inside the actual email history — that's where the evidence is scattered, and the one place a generic assistant never looks with this purpose.

**Block 12 — Close (~8s)**
> What Remains. Built with Streamlit, OpenRouter, and Exa. Code is on GitHub — link in the description.

---

## Total estimated runtime
~140 seconds of narration across 12 blocks. If your silent footage runs shorter, trim blocks 2 and 11 first (they're context, not demo-critical) to stay under the 2-minute hackathon limit — blocks 3 through 10 are the ones a judge needs to see and hear.

## Reminder before exporting
- Never show or narrate over the "Connect a Gmail account" button — only the "Try a sample inbox" path.
- Say sponsor names explicitly (OpenRouter, Exa) somewhere in the final cut — required by the hackathon rules.
- Keep total video length at or under 2:00.
